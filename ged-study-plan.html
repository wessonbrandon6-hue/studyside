How to study effectively

1. Active recall

Close your notes and explain the idea from memory. Then check what you missed.

2. Keep an error log

For every wrong answer, write the correct method and why your first answer was wrong.

3. Practise under time

Build up from short timed sets to longer practice tests as your confidence grows.

4. Protect your rest

Consistent sleep and breaks help learning stick. A sustainable plan beats an exhausting one.
